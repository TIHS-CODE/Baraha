Pusoy Dos

Play

A card game where the entire deck is split between the players and they take turns dropping card/s that beat the previous card combo.
First to run out of cards wins. Where I am from we play till last player wins.

4 Players recommended.
Suit Hierarchy: Diamonds > Hearts > Spades > Clubs.
Rank Hierarchy: 2 > A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > ... > 4 > 3.
Player with 3 of Clubs starts the game. They can use any card combo as long as it uses the 3 of Clubs.
Standard Card Combos. Check the Wikipedia for card combinations and lore.
Check Settings for card customization. For Phones I prefer Large Card Width. For PC I prefer Compact Card Width
If playing with less than 4 players you can either set the player count as needed or leave it at 4 and skip the hand/s with no player.
